# Playing a Saturday on Linden Row

The player's immediate concern is getting Noor and her painted sign out of the flat and meeting Mara for their Saturday walk. The longer question is what it means to keep showing up for someone who might prefer a closed door. The bakery gives the ensemble a place to meet without arranging a clinical encounter.

**Opening benchmark, proposed.** Begin with Sami switching on the bakery lights across from Noor. Give Noor control while she can still see the street. At the easel she can choose a particular window and place a mark, set the brush down or go straight out. Carry that visible mark into the epilogue. A brush rinsed in a jar and a wet cuff can do work now assigned to an explanatory sentence. Give the sign a resting place by the exit and a visible destination at the library. No compulsory checklist.

**Structure.** Keep the seven existing chapters and six locations. Use the shared landing twice with different practical aims. Keep unrestricted movement and nonspatial action choices in each perspective. The existing renderer, chapter registration and independent build already support this. Prototype one activity before contemplating an engine replacement.
