# Art direction and shot list

Use the existing warm wood, muted walls and saturated personal belongings as the starting palette. Preserve the striped awning and Dele’s gold lettering as street anchors. Direct attention through prop placement, silhouette and light. Preserve character identity in grayscale and at phone size.

|Set|Existing evidence|Next authored detail and shot test|
|---|---|---|
|Linden Row and park edge|Awnings, bus, bench and apartment windows share one street; distant buildings read as flat silhouettes.|Frame bakery opening and window in one shot. Give the peeling F a visible edge and test its readability on a phone.|
|Noor’s flat|Easel beside the bed, books and sign; left wall cropped on portrait captures.|Place paint rag and working study within reach. Frame wrist, easel and exit during painting.|
|Landing|Two numbered doors, runner, bike, railing and noticeboard.|Test sightline to the light under 2C from 2B; revisit with changed daylight and the same repair left undone.|
|Dele’s flat|Sofa, small table, lamp and tools; night isolates the lamp.|Give the tools a storage pattern consistent with a signwriter. Inspect tea, note and pastry placement independently.|
|Linden Library|Shelves, cart, desk and empty sign wall.|Use wear on the bench and a visible change to the cart. Frame a shelf and the relevant hand together.|
|Walt and June’s house|Kitchen table and workshop share a set. The birthday chair affords a concrete joint activity.|Show wood grain and the unfinished armrest at working scale. Keep Walt’s hand and Mara’s paper clear of dialogue.|

Existing figures use projected anchors and a shared pose solver. First author contact poses for brush, seat and cup, then capture approach, contact, hold and release. Separate portrait/landscape comparison frames at the same action beat. No replacement renderer is justified by the current stills.
