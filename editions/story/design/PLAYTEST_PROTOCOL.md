# First-time, representation and access sessions

No sessions have been recorded. Recruit consenting adults separately for first-time play, paid lived-experience consultation, and physical assistive-technology testing. Agree on compensation, recording choice, retention and withdrawal before starting. Use private participant records; publish only consented aggregates or redacted findings.

**First-time session.** Start at the title with a clean Story save. Ask the player to play a Saturday on Linden Row. Do not name a philosophical concept or prescribe a route. Observe first self-directed action, hesitation at the landing, interruptions, reason to continue, attempt to resume and interpretation of the last window. Let players decline or stop. Ask “What did Noor see herself?”, “Which choice mattered later?” and “Was there a moment when the game put words in your mouth?” Accept uncertainty and silence.

**Paid editorial session.** Review a chosen scene in context. Offer captions, transcript or skipping. Ask where the scene assumes knowledge, where the character has purposes of their own, and where a line feels imposed. No personal diagnosis or disclosure is required. Record the exact route before attributing an issue.

**Physical access session.** Record device, OS, browser, input and settings with consent. From a clean title try one object, one conversation, content note, pause, reload and exit. Observe focus order, labels, announcement collisions, touch reach, magnified reflow and the ability to leave. Offer breaks. Do not infer disability from a missed input.

For each issue retain build SHA, route, setting, observation, impact and reproduction steps. Separate direct observation, participant interpretation and proposed change. Compare the next build on the same route; return editorial wording to the reviewer only by prior agreement.
